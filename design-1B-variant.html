Official Biography & Press Kit

General Mamba

General Mamba is a New York Times bestselling essayist, memoirist, and novelist whose work examines memory, faith, Black manhood, and the moral architecture of community.

His acclaimed memoir, The Weight & The Grace, received a Whiting Award in Nonfiction and was named a Best Book of the Year by NPR and The Atlantic. His essay collections—including Born Bold, The Next Chapter, and The Unseen Bridge—are taught in universities nationwide.

In speculative fiction, he is the creator of The Twitch Universe trilogy (The First Tremor, High Voltage, and Still Waters). He divides his time between Brooklyn, New York, and coastal South Carolina.
