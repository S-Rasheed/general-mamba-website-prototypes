001 • Memoir 2024

The Weight & The Grace

A Memoir of Faith, Fatherhood & Southern Heritage

A deeply moving memoir examining fatherhood, Southern pulpit heritage, and the quiet grace required to set heavy burdens down. Across three generations in coastal South Carolina and Brooklyn, General Mamba traces how silence shapes a family—and what happens when we finally speak.

002 • Essays 2025

The Next Chapter

Twelve Essays on Renewal & Civic Courage

Twelve luminous essays on reinvention, civic courage, and building enduring institutions in uncertain times. Written between front-porch conversations and late-night train rides, these meditations ask what communities owe the generations waiting just beyond the door.

003 • Cultural Prose 2023

Born Bold

Portraits of Quiet Visionaries & Artists

Intimate literary portraits of artists, choir directors, teachers, and neighborhood builders who refused to shrink to fit the room. Winner of the Whiting Award in Nonfiction, Born Bold celebrates the unheralded architects of American cultural life.

004 • Narrative History 2021

The Unseen Bridge

Coastal South Carolina & The Great Migration

Tracing the hidden generational links between Lowcountry tidal towns and northern industrial cities—told through handwritten letters, church ledgers, and family recipes carried northward in cardboard suitcases.

005 • Epistolary 2022

Letters to a Young Sinner

Epistles on Doubt, Art & Moral Imagination

Written in the tradition of Baldwin and Rilke, twenty-four candid letters addressed to a young writer wrestling with inherited doctrine, creative ambition, and the discipline of mercy.

006 • Forthcoming Prose Spring 2027

Sunday Sermons & Salt

A Meditation on Tide, Table & Spoken Word

A lyrical study of coastal sanctuaries, Sunday dinner tables, and the cadence of Southern oratory—exploring how food, sea air, and spoken rhythm preserve memory across centuries.

⚡ The Twitch Universe • Three-Volume Saga

An Electric Chronicle in Three Movements

Follow the chronological arc of General Mamba’s speculative thriller trilogy—set along a fractured coastal power grid where aging copper lines store human voices and every blackout demands a reckoning.

I
Out Now • Volume I 2024 Release

The First Tremor

When a November substation collapse plunges three coastal wards into darkness, sound archivist Darius Cole discovers that aging copper lines are replaying human voices from twenty years earlier.

II
Out Now • Book II National Bestseller

High Voltage

Pursued from Charleston to Baltimore, the underground sound-keepers of the Copper Choir race to decode a second surge before GridCorp permanently erases the Atlantic corridor.

The Copper Choir: Church audio engineers and pirate radio operators preserving unedited neighborhood oral histories.

Key Characters: Darius Cole, Miriam Mamba-Bell, and former lineman Deacon Ezra.

III
Coming Soon • Trilogy Finale Fall 2026

Still Waters

In the sweeping conclusion to The Twitch Universe, every buried frequency converges on a single midnight reckoning over who owns the city’s past—and who gets to write its future.
