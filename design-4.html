The Twitch Universe Trilogy

Three interconnected novels. One city learning what happens when buried memories surge back onto the grid.

Beginning with The First Tremor, continuing through High Voltage, and culminating in Still Waters, General Mamba blends speculative suspense with intimate portraits of Black family, sound archives, and survival.

Biography • Brooklyn & South Carolina

He writes what men are told not to say.

General Mamba is the creator of The Twitch Universe trilogy and author of The Weight of Grace. His essays have appeared in The Atlantic, Harper’s Magazine, and The Paris Review.

A former high school literature teacher and recipient of a Whiting Award in Nonfiction, he divides his time between Brooklyn and the South Carolina Lowcountry.
